## Active Working Groups

### Subjective Methods

Collaborative efforts to improve subjective video quality test methods.

- Multimedia Experience and Human Factors ([MEHF](/projects/mehf "MEHF"))
- Quality Assessment for Health Applications ([QAH](/projects/qah "Quality Assessment for Health Applications (QAH)"))
- Statistical Analysis Methods ([SAM](/projects/sam "Statistical Analysis Methods (SAM)"))
- Subjective and objective assessment of GenAI content ([SOGAI](/projects/sogai "Subjective and objective assessment of GenAI content (SOGAI)"))

### Objective Metrics

Developing and validating objective video quality metrics.

- No Reference Metrics ([NORM project](/projects/norm "No Reference Metrics (NORM)"), [NORM resources](/projects/norm-resources "NORM Resources"))
- Implementer's Guide for Video Quality Metrics ([IGVQM](/projects/igvqm "Implementer’s Guide to Video Quality Metrics (IGVQM)"))
- [JEG-Hybrid](https://vqegjeg.github.io/jeg-hybrid/)
- Quality Assessment for Computer Vision Applications ([QACoViA](/projects/qacovia "QACoViA"))

## Completed Validation Tests

- [FRTV Phase I](/projects/frtv-phase-i "FRTV Phase-I") - completed June, 2000
- [FRTV Phase II](/projects/frtv-phase-ii "FRTV Phase-II") - completed August 25, 2003
- [Multimedia](/projects/multimedia "Multimedia Phase I") Phase I - completed September 12, 2008
- [RRNR-TV](/projects/rrnr-tv "RRNR-TV") - completed June 22, 2009
- [HDTV](/projects/hdtv "HDTV") Phase I - completed June 30, 2010
- [Audiovisual Quality Integration (AVHD-AV)](/projects/audiovisual-quality-integration "Audiovisual Quality Integration") - completed in 2012 by concluding that "*<a href="https://vqeg.org/publications/details?pub=2565" rel="noopener" target="_blank" data-anchor="?pub=2565">audio-only MOS × video-only MOS = audio-visual MOS</a>"*
- [Hybrid Perceptual/Bitstream](/projects/hybrid-perceptual-bitstream/hybrid "Hybrid Perceptual Bitstream") - completed September 12, 2014
- [AVHD-AS / P.NATS Phase 2](/projects/avhd-as-pnats-phase-2 "AVHD-AS / P.NATS Phase 2") - completed January 2020

## Dormant Projects

- HDTV Phase II and Multimedia Phase II were merged to create [Audiovisaul HD](/projects/avhd "Audiovisual HD") in December, 2012.
- [3DTV](/projects/3dtv "3DTV") was closed March 2016, and the remaining 3DTV projects moved into the new Immersive Media Group.
- [VLQA](/projects/vlqa "Visually Lossless Compression") (Visually Lossless Quality Analysis) was closed November 2017.
- <a href="/projects/vime" target="_parent" title="VIME">VIME</a> (Video and Image Models for consumer content Evaluation) was closed November 2017 and replaced with the No Reference Metric (NORM) working group.
- [MOAVI](/projects/moavi "MOAVI") (Monitoring of Audio Visual Quality by Key Indicators) was closed January 2018. Work continues in the No Reference Metric (NORM) working group.
- [QART](/projects/qart "QART") (Quality Assessment for Recognition Tasks) was closed November 2017. Work continues in the new No Reference Metric Group.
- [Ultra HD](/projects/ultra-hd "Ultra HD") was closed March 2018. Work continues in other VQEG working groups, since 4K monitors are widely available. 
- [HDR/WCG](/projects/hdr "HDR") (High Dynamic Range Video / Wide Color Gamut) was closed March 2018. Work continues in other VQEG working groups. 
- [Independent Lab Group (ILG)](/projects/ilg "ILG") - the ILG was closed due to the lack of need for independent oversight of VQEG validation efforts.
- [PsyPhyQA](/projects/psyphyqa "PsyPhyQA") (Psycho-Physiological Quality Assessment) was suspended December 2022, until interest on this topic resumes.
- Audiovisual HD ([AVHD](/projects/avhd "Audiovisual HD")) was suspended in November 2024.
- Computer Generated Imagery ([CGI](/projects/cgi "Computer Generated Imagery (CGI)")) was suspended in November 2024.
