### VQEG Tools and Subjective Labs Setup

The main goal of the VQEG Tools and Subjective Labs Setup support group is to provide the research community with a wide variety of software tools and guidance in order to facilitate video quality research.

- <a href="http://vqegstl.ugent.be" target="_blank" data-router-slot="disabled" rel="noopener" type="external" title="VQEG Tools and Subjective Labs setup website">VQEG Tools and Subjective Labs Setup website</a>

### VQEG Software Tools

VQEG publishes open source software for video quality assessment, subjective testing, and streaming simulation. More projects are available on [GitHub](https://github.com/VQEG).

- [Software Tools and Labs Setup](/software-tools/): a collection of software to prepare, conduct, and analyze subjective tests
- [Number of Subjects Calculator](/number-of-subjects/): calculate the required number of subjects for subjective quality tests ([source code](https://github.com/VQEG/number-of-subjects))
- [SI/TI Tools](https://github.com/VQEG/siti-tools): calculate spatial and temporal information (SI/TI) of video sequences
- [DOI Metadata Generator](/doi/): generate HTML landing pages and Crossref XML for DOI registration ([source code](https://github.com/VQEG/doi))
- [tpkloss](https://github.com/VQEG/tpkloss): Telchemy packet loss insertion tool for network impairment simulation
- [streamsim](https://github.com/VQEG/streamsim): a streaming simulation toolkit for video
- [T.35 Quality Metadata](https://github.com/VQEG/t35-quality-metadata): specification and registration for ITU-T T.35 quality metadata

### VQEG Reports from Completed Validation Tests

VQEG has completed the following validation tests of objective video quality models. Each test involved a group of proponents who submitted models for evaluation. The independent lab group (ILG) implemented the test and ensured a fair and unbiased evaluation of models.

Where possible, the videos and individual subject ratings from these validation tests are made available on the Consumer Digital Video Library (CDVL, <a href="https://www.cdvl.org/" target="_blank" data-router-slot="disabled" rel="noopener" type="external">www.cdvl.org</a>), for research and development purposes. Some VQEG datasets cannot be redistributed due to licensing restrictions on the source content.

- **AVHD-AS/P.NATS Phase 2** was a joint project of VQEG and ITU-T Study Group 12 Question 14. The goal was to develop a multitude of objective models, varying in terms of complexity/type of input/use-cases for the assessment of video quality in adaptive bitrate streaming services over reliable transport up to 4K. For more information, see the <a href="/projects/avhd-as-pnats-phase-2" target="" data-router-slot="disabled" title="AVHD-AS / P.NATS Phase 2">AVHD</a> project page.

- **Hybrid Perceptual Bitstream (Hybrid)** examined the performance of Hybrid models for VGA/WVGA and HDTV, using ACR-HR. Ten datasets were created. The test plan, final report, and status of ITU Recommendations are available on the <a href="/projects/hybrid-perceptual-bitstream/hybrid" target="" data-router-slot="disabled" title="Hybrid Perceptual Bitstream">Hybrid project page</a>.

- **High Definition Television (HDTV)** examined the performance of FR, RR and NR models for HDTV, using ACR-HR. Six datasets were created, five of which can be redistributed for research and development purposes. The test plan, final report, video sequences, and resulting ITU Recommendations are available on the <a href="/projects/hdtv" target="" data-router-slot="disabled" title="HDTV">HDTV Phase I</a> project page.

- **Reduced Reference/No Reference Television (RRNR-TV)** examined the performance of RR and NR models on standard definition video, using ACR-HR. Two datasets were created: 525-line (NTSC) and 625-line (PAL) video. The test plan, final report, and resulting ITU Recommendations are available on the <a href="/projects/rrnr-tv" target="" data-router-slot="disabled" title="RRNR-TV">RRNR-TV</a> project page.

- **Multimedia (MM) Phase I** examined the performance of FR, RR and NR models for VGA, CIF and QCIF video (no audio), using ACR-HR. 41 datasets were created, none of which can be redistributed. The test plan, final report and resulting ITU Recommendations are available on the <a href="/projects/multimedia" target="" data-router-slot="disabled" title="Multimedia Phase I">Multimedia Phase I</a> project page.

- **Full Reference Television (FRTV) Phase II** examined the performance of FR and NR models on standard definition video, using the DSCQS methodology. Two datasets were created: 525-line (NTSC) and 625-line (PAL) video. The test plan, final report, and resulting ITU Recommendations are available on the <a href="/projects/frtv-phase-ii" target="" data-router-slot="disabled" title="FRTV Phase-II">FR-TV Phase II</a> project page. The video sequences cannot be distributed due to licensing restrictions.

- **Full Reference Television (FRTV) Phase I** examined the performance of FR and NR models on standard definition video, using the DSCQS methodology. Four datasets were created: high quality and medium quality, for both 525-line (NTSC) and 625-line (PAL) video. Models were difficult to distinguish due to the narrow range of quality in each dataset. The test plan, final report, and video sequences are available on the <a href="/projects/frtv-phase-i" target="" data-router-slot="disabled" title="FRTV Phase-I">FR-TV Phase I project</a> project page.
